Modifications: reverse lookup from English glosses, separate comma/semicolon alternatives outside parentheses,
normalize whitespace and punctuation, remove infinitive prefix 'to', deduplicate identical Korean equivalents,
and add the seven sample expressions listed in supplement.json. The source typo '공선 변호인' for
'public defender' is excluded from the derived index and replaced by '국선 변호인'. Original TSV is unchanged.

This is an unreviewed community dataset, not an Oxford, Cambridge or Collins dictionary.
Korean equivalents are reverse lookup candidates, not exhaustive or guaranteed definitions.
No AI or remote translation service is used during searches. Search queries are not stored or uploaded.
